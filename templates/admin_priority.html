Transparent Decision-Support Architecture (Guide Section 8):
The priority score is calculated using deterministic Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) across six criteria: Occupancy (20%), Demographic Vulnerability (25%), Structural Damage (20%), Access Difficulty (15%), Waiting Time (10%, capped at 72h), and Escalation Flag (10%). Every calculation run archives the exact weights into priority_runs.weights_json for auditability.
